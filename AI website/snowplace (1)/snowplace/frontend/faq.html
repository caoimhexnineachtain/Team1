Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Got a question? We've answered the most common ones below.

What is Snow Place Like Home?▾
Snow Place Like Home is a 2D endless-runner style game where you control a penguin trying to return to the Arctic after being taken away by a cowboy villain. You travel through three distinct environments — desert, jungle and wild west farm — avoiding obstacles, collecting ice cubes and surviving the journey home.
Who is the main character?▾
The main character is a brave penguin who has been taken far from his Arctic home. Despite the challenges ahead — a scorching desert, a dense jungle and a wild west farm — he is determined to make it back.
Who is the villain?▾
The villain is a cowboy who snatched the penguin from the Arctic. He follows the penguin throughout the game and is always just behind. The penguin must outrun him and navigate three dangerous worlds to escape.
What are the three levels?▾
The three levels are: Level 1 – Desert (Easy), Level 2 – Jungle (Medium) and Level 3 – Wild West Farm (Hard). Each environment has its own obstacles, atmosphere and difficulty. Visit the Levels page for full details.
What are ice cubes used for?▾
Ice cubes are collectables scattered throughout each level. They represent pieces of the penguin's Arctic home and add points to your score when collected. The more ice cubes you collect, the higher your score — especially important in Endless Mode.
What is Endless Mode?▾
Endless Mode removes the level structure and lets you run indefinitely. Obstacles keep spawning, the game gradually gets faster and your score increases the longer you survive. There is no finish line — the goal is simply to beat your own record. Visit the Endless Mode page to submit your score to the leaderboard.
How do I play the game?▾
Use the arrow keys to move left and right, and press SPACE to jump over obstacles. Your penguin moves automatically — your job is to react to what's coming. See the How to Play page for a full guide including tips for beginners.
What happens when I run out of health?▾
When the penguin's health reaches zero, the Game Over screen appears showing your final score and ice cube count. You can then choose to restart the current level or return to the main menu.
How many developers made this game?▾
Snow Place Like Home was created by a team of four student developers who each contributed different areas — game design and programming, graphics and art, web development and story/testing. Visit the About Us page to meet the team.
What technology was the game built with?▾
The game was built using Python and the Pygame library. The website was built using HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the front-end, with a Node.js/Express back-end connected to a SQLite database.
How do I leave a review?▾
Visit the Reviews page, choose your star rating, enter your name and write your review. You can also optionally select your favourite level. Your review will appear on the page after submission.
How do I submit my Endless Mode score?▾
After finishing an Endless Mode run, visit the Endless Mode page and enter your name, score, ice cubes collected and survival time in the submission form. Your score will appear on the leaderboard.
How do I contact the developers?▾
Visit the Contact page and fill in the form with your name, email, subject and message. We aim to respond within a few days.
Is the game free to play?▾
Yes! Snow Place Like Home is a free student project game. It was created as part of a game development course and is available to play without any cost.
