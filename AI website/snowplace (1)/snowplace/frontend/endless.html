The Rules of Endless

No levels. No checkpoints. Just you, the penguin, and an ever-accelerating world.

🏃
Keep Running

The penguin never stops. Obstacles keep coming from all three environments mixed together.

📈
Speed Increases

Every 30 seconds the game gets faster. What starts easy quickly becomes frantic.

🧊
Collect Ice Cubes

Ice cubes keep spawning throughout. Every one you grab adds to your score.

❤️
Limited Health

You start with the same health as normal mode. Hits hurt — and there's no finish line to reach.

Submit Your Score
