The Rules of Endless

No levels. No checkpoints. Just you, the penguin, and a world with decreased gravity.

🏃
Keep Running

The penguin never stops, avoid the obstacles for as long as possible.

🧊
Collect Ice Cubes

Ice cubes keep spawning throughout. Every one you grab adds to your score.

❤️

You start with the same health as normal mode. Hits hurt — and there's no finish line to reach.
