Game Mechanics

How It All Works

🏃

Movement

The penguin runs automatically. You don't control direction — your only job is to jump over what's coming.

⬆️

Jumping

Press SPACE to jump. The penguin will leap into the air, clearing obstacles on the ground. Time your jump carefully — too early or too late and you'll take a hit. You cannot jump while already in the air.

🧊

Collecting Ice Cubes

Ice cubes appear scattered throughout each level. Run through them to collect them automatically. Each ice cube adds points to your score. The more you collect, the higher you rank on the leaderboard.

🚧

Avoiding Obstacles

Obstacles appear at ground level and must be jumped over. Some levels introduce airborne hazards too. Hitting an obstacle doesn't end the game immediately — it reduces your health. Multiple hits will end your run.

❤️

Health System

The penguin starts each level with a set amount of health (displayed at the top of the screen). Each collision with an obstacle removes one health point. When all health is gone, the game is over. Stay sharp!

💀

Game Over

When the penguin's health reaches zero, the Game Over screen appears. Your final score and ice cube count are displayed.

🏆

Scoring

Your score increases over time as you survive. Collecting ice cubes gives you bonus points. Completing a level gives you a completion bonus. The harder the level, the more points you earn per second.

📈

Increasing Difficulty

Each level is harder than the last — obstacles appear faster, gaps are tighter and the cowboy gets closer. In Endless Mode the difficulty continuously increases the longer you survive. Stay focused!
