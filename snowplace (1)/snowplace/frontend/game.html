The Story

A Penguin's Journey Home

Deep in the Arctic, a penguin lived a peaceful life — until a mischievous cowboy decided to take him far, far away from home.

Now our penguin must travel through three treacherous environments — a scorching desert, a dense jungle and a wild west farm — to find his way back. Along the way he must dodge obstacles, collect precious ice cubes and stay one step ahead of the cowboy who is hot on his trail.

Every step brings him closer to the Arctic. Every obstacle tries to stop him. Can you get him home?

How to Play It

Snow Place Like Home is a desktop game built in Python. It runs on your computer — not in the browser.

1️⃣

Install Python

Download and install Python 3 from python.org if you don't have it already.

2️⃣

Install Pygame

Open a terminal and run: pip install pygame

3️⃣

Download the Game

Download the project from our GitHub repository and unzip it.

4️⃣

Run the Game

Open a terminal in the project folder and run: python Leah/level_1.py

🐧

The game cannot be played inside a web browser — it needs Python and Pygame installed on your machine. Once you've run it once, it starts instantly every time.

🧊 Ice Cubes — Why They Matter

Ice cubes are scattered throughout each level. To a penguin who misses the Arctic, they're a reminder of home — and they're valuable. Collect as many as you can to boost your score and prove you're worthy of the Arctic.
